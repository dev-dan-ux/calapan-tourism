Essential Tourist Tips

Make the most of your stay in Calapan

Best Times to Visit

  • Dry Season: November to May (Best weather)
  • Peak Tourist Season: December to April
  • Rainy Season: June to October (Fewer tourists, lower rates)
  • Local Festivals: January (Feast of Santo Niño)

Internet & Connectivity

  • Major telcos: Smart, Globe, DITO
  • Vista Mall has free WiFi
  • Most hotels/restaurants offer WiFi
  • Mobile signal is generally good in city proper

Payment Methods

  • Cash is king - most places cash only
  • Major malls accept credit/debit cards
  • GCash and PayMaya widely accepted
  • Always carry small bills (₱20, ₱50, ₱100)

Local Language

  • Primary: Tagalog
  • Also spoken: English (widely understood)
  • Greeting: "Mabuhay" or "Kumusta"
  • Thank you: "Salamat"
  • Most tourist areas have English-speaking staff

Important Reminders

Health & Safety
  • Bring insect repellent
  • Wear sunscreen
  • Stay hydrated
  • Drink bottled water only
Cultural Respect
  • Dress modestly in churches
  • Ask permission before taking photos
  • Respect local customs
  • Be polite and patient
Travel Smart
  • Book accommodations in advance
  • Keep copies of documents
  • Register with your embassy if long stay
  • Get travel insurance

Transportation & Rates

Current rates to help you budget and avoid overcharging

Standard Transportation Rates (2024-2025)

Important: These are the standard rates approved by local authorities. Always confirm the fare before boarding to avoid misunderstandings.
Vehicle Type Base Fare Additional Rate Notes
Tricycle ₱15.00 ₱2.00 per km after first 2km Maximum 4 passengers. Special trip: ₱80-150 depending on distance
Jeepney ₱13.00 ₱1.50 per km Within city proper. Student/Senior discount available
Mini Bus ₱15.00 - ₱25.00 Depends on route For longer routes to nearby municipalities
Tricycle (Pier to City) ₱100.00 - ₱150.00 Special trip rate Negotiable for groups. Shared ride: ₱30-50 per person
Habal-habal (Motorcycle) ₱20.00 - ₱50.00 Depends on distance For hard-to-reach areas only. Maximum 2 passengers
Transportation Tips:
  • Always ask for the meter or agree on the fare before starting your trip
  • Keep small bills (₱20, ₱50, ₱100) for easier transactions
  • During peak hours (7-9 AM, 5-7 PM), fares may be higher
  • Rainy weather may result in slightly higher rates
  • Book through ride-hailing apps when available for transparent pricing
